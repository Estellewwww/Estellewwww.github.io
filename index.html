Open the methodology & limitations +

This is a deliberately simplified insurance demonstration, not an investment or insurance quote. Initial vehicle prices, segment mix and depreciation assumptions are invented. Each vehicle’s reference maturity value follows annual compound depreciation (compact ICE 14%, SUV ICE 12%, BEV 20%). The guarantee is 95% of that reference value.

Claim = max(guaranteed maturity value − simulated resale value, 0)

Market and BEV shocks shift reference resale values. Normally distributed errors combine a shared market factor and vehicle-specific noise (40% common-factor variance). Volatility is a percentage of reference maturity value. Resale values are floored at zero. We simulate 400 outcomes for 600 vehicles using a fixed seed. Expected claims are the sample mean; the 95th percentile is an empirical quantile of total claims. Expenses are assumed at 1% of initial portfolio value for the full term. No discounting, recoveries, mileage model, claim timing, adverse selection, parameter estimation or regulatory capital model is included. The finite simulations and assumed normal errors are limitations, especially for extreme tail risk. Vehicle segments are illustrative and not estimates of current markets.

Changing premium affects loss ratio and margin, but does not change vehicle values or expected claims. Changing BEV count share also changes total initial portfolio value because the synthetic segments have different prices.
